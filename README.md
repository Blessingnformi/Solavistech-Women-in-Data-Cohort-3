Solavistech Women in Data is a community that brings together women who are eager to explore and grow in the fields of data and technology, which have traditionally been male-dominated. Through training, mentorship, and peer support, the programme empowers women with the skills and confidence needed to thrive in these fields and help bridge the gender gap in technology.


Coming from an accounting and finance background, I recognise the growing role of data and technology in improving financial reporting, budgeting, risk management, and decision-making. This has sparked my interest in developing data analysis skills that will complement my existing financial expertise and enable me to adapt to the evolving demands of the profession. 

Through the Solavistech Women in Data programme, I hope to build a strong foundation in data analysis, gain practical experience with data tools and platforms, and explore innovative ways to apply these skills to financial management and reporting.

I therefore, aspire to leverage the power of data and technology to enhance my professional capabilities and contribute to more efficient, informed, and data-driven financial decision-making.
